# LazySheep Cross-border FAQ (EN)

## Return window
For most apparel items, returns are accepted within **7 days after delivery**, unused and with original packaging.
Final sale items (e.g. socks marked as final sale) are **not eligible** for no-reason returns.

## Refund timeline
After the warehouse receives and inspects the return, refunds are usually issued within **3–7 business days** to the original payment method.
AI agents cannot process refunds directly — a human supervisor must approve high-risk refund requests.

## Exchange / replacement
Damaged or wrong-item shipments can request a **replacement**. Create an approval request with order number and proof photos when available.

## Customs & tariffs
International orders may incur **duties and taxes** charged by the destination country. These fees are typically paid by the customer unless the product page states DDP (delivered duty paid).
If a package is held by customs, provide the tracking number and we will share the carrier’s customs contact guidance.

## Shipping SLA (reference)
- Domestic CN: 2–5 days after shipment
- Cross-border economy: 7–15 business days
- Express: 3–7 business days (subject to customs clearance)

## Address change
Address changes are only possible **before the warehouse ships**. After shipment, please contact the carrier or request human handoff.
