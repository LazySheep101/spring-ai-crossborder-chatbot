# Return policy
Hoodies can be returned within 7 days after delivery. Final-sale items such as socks cannot be returned. A return only creates a pending approval; the refund is not paid until a supervisor approves it.

# Customs duty
Import duty on cross-border orders is paid by the buyer. If customs holds the parcel, provide the order number so support can check the clearance documents.

# Shipping time
US lanes take about 7-15 business days. EU lanes take about 10-20 business days. After the order is shipped, tracking can be looked up by order number.
